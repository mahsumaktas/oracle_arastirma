Gunun Ozeti

#

Bugunun ana ekseni agent ve model yarismasinin "demo"dan runtime, compliance ve operasyonel maliyet katmanina kaymasi oldu. OpenAI GPT-Live icin turnless voice mimarisini anlatti; Alibaba Qwen3.8-Max ile open-weight cephede frontier sinyal verdi; AWS Bedrock ise agentic data ops ve formal policy refinement tarafini guclendirdi. Guvenlikte yeni hikaye "AI daha fazla bug buluyor" degil, "AI bulgu hacmi ve agent davranisi governance'i zorluyor" oldu: IBM access-control eksigini, The Register CVE pipeline slop sorununu, Docker ise agent kararlarinin SIEM'e akan audit log ihtiyacini one cikardi. Kaynaklar: https://the-decoder.com/ibm-finds-92-of-companies-hit-by-ai-security-breaches-lacked-basic-access-controls/ ve https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/03/ai-slop-pollutes-the-cve-pipeline-with-fake-vulns/5282462 ve https://www.docker.com/blog/docker-ai-governance-audit-logs-now-where-your-security-team-already-works/
