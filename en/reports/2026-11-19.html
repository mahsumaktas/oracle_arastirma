Sections 01

Summary of the Day

#

The main signal of the night is that the ability of the agent and the reliability of the environment in which it is operated should be evaluated separately. Malicious agent-framework versions from Microsoft's actual PyPI publisher indicate that the package name and previous clean versions are not enough. The Codex sandbox article clarifies that the command, file, network and MCP server are not within the same boundary. On the other hand, small models, long mission training, world production and regional inference become more feasible; However, 1M token training session, 1M context, full-batch throughput, live speech delay, and PPA improvement do not mean that the entire chip accelerates at the same rate. This paragraph is editorial synthesis. Package analysis · Sandbox · DaisyRose · Rumble
