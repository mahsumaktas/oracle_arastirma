Summary of the Day

#

The main axis of this night is the separation of information, calculation and authority layers to which the agent connects. Hugging Face moves language model routing to OpenRouter, while AWS combines table-to-table calculation with text retrieval; It also says that the tenant instruction has no data access limit. NVIDIA's SC26 announcements expand scientific agents' infrastructure to GPU, network and quantum resources; Today's product and the 2028 plan, verified results and provisional measurement are not the same thing. OpenAI's science access and embedded review agreements are important, but should not be read as gaining per-investigator access or independent security clearance. Hugging Face · AWS tenant guide · NVIDIA Rubin HPC · OpenAI reviews

  • 9. Scientists' use of ChatGPT: productivity estimate, not causal experiment. OpenAI discloses the classification of 580,186 scientific messages from 1,819 scientists and survey responses from 251 people during the period August 20–September 22. The authors attempt to improve probabilistic consistency and accuracy together with a supervisory model that uses justifications of correlated forecasts. Abstract reports a 9.3% improvement in Brier score and an 82–90% reduction in structural inconsistency metrics for the retrieval-augmented GPT-5.1 forecaster on ForecastBench. Comment: Single benchmark and author experiments; not financial forecast earnings or results for all models. Full experiments were not rerun. Seen on November 16 academic stream; main submission 13 November; abstract level HIGH/MED. Original preprint and history
  • 11. DECODE — November 13 preprint: controlled intervention in explaining the score. In five synthetic collection environments with known dynamics, the planning horizon and what information is accessed are changed separately. The authors found that intervention effects depended on the sequence and response model used; It specifically states that the loss cannot be apportioned to individual causes. Comment: The benefit of longer planning is not the result of giving each agent more turns; Testing with stable solver and clear information conditions is required. Abstract/submission history read; no independent repetition. Original preprint
