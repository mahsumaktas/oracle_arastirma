#

  • E07 — NariQ: agent traces in quantization calibration. Trellis-coded post-training quantization is recommended with tokens produced by the BF16 model in real agent sessions. 3.0 and 2.5 bit/weight on Qwen3.8-27B claims to maintain BF16 level in AIME/RealWorldQA as well as DeepSWE and Terminal Bench; 2.0 bits behind.   • E08 — SKILL.PNG: moving the instruction to the optimized image instead of text. Frozen produces pixel-space skill by taking gradient from VLM's vision encoder. Beating textual skill methods compared in 9 out of 16 settings in four VLMs/four benchmarks; Inference reports using 64–256 image tokens instead of approximately 900–2000 text tokens. Limit: Transfer within Ensemble is not a guarantee of reliable operation in any unseen VLM. Fewer tokens do not mean universal lower cost or security superiority. First submission 19 November; abstract HIGH, preprint. Study
  • E09 — Exact state tracking: reads the language model, calculates external registers. Frozen reader extracts event transitions; Order dependent composition is done in the outer integer register. 298/299 on the new machine misses the targeted 0.99 one-sided lower confidence bound; In free-form issue comments, the result is 68.8%, frequency baseline 61.0%. Regex+executor also resolved fixed-format control documents. Comment: Success in structured long flow should not be exaggerated as general natural language event-understanding accuracy. First submission 18 November; abstract HIGH, preprint. Study
  • E10 — METR Q4: transition from single horizon number to mission profile. New TH2; It separates verifiable coding/optimization from judgment tasks that do not have explicit empirical feedback. Horizon doubling in TH1.1+TH2 combination 3.61 months; 95% CI 2.08–4.94. Estimated horizon up to 27 hours is described for the upper model in the verifiable subset of TH2; This does not mean that the agent works 27 hours a day without interruption. Model-judges can score similar/strong model outputs more than expert scorers. Limit: Task selection, human baseline, reward hacking and lifecycle-cost estimation are part of the comment. November 20; actual method/results sections HIGH, not universal workforce measurement. Original assessment
