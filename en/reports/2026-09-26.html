KAT-2 - Research & Papers

#
  • EvoTreeNAD recommends genealogy-guided evolution for LLM-driven neural architecture discovery. On the neural architecture search side, the combination of LLM agent + evolutionary family tree should be followed. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29016
  • Speculative Evaluation of Stochastic LLMs focuses on the problem of reducing benchmark cost. Stochastic rollouts are expensive; Paper is looking for an evaluation method that will use repetition more efficiently. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28560
  • FLEET aims to produce enhanced trajectories from logits entropy. Connecting the entropy signal to trajectory selection instead of temperature sampling may be practical for LLM response stability. Source: https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.27657
  • GeoPair preserves cross-layer geometry for training-free transformer compression. In post-training compression, it tries to preserve the common geometry instead of optimizing the layers in isolation. Source: https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.25963
  • WhatWorkedBench measures whether AI research agents understand the effect of the experiment. It is critical for the agent to keep accurate "what worked" information for self-improvement. Source: https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.27490
