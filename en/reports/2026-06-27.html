Summary of the Day

#

The main axis of today was the politicization of model access, the security of the agent infrastructure and self-improving/multimodal agent architectures on the research side. As OpenAI announced GPT-5.6 Sol, the "trusted partner" access model originating from the US government became the biggest regulatory signal of the day. On the agent side, Vercel Eve, Dapr verifiable execution, AWS Agent Toolkit and MCP poisoning studies accelerated the transition from "agent app" to "agent control plane". On the research side, Red Queen Godel Machine, Qwen-Image-Agent, ProtoKV and ShareLock gave high signals.
