Sections 01

Daily synthesis

#

The common question is whether recognizing evidence also produces the right state assessment and action. Siren separates reaching outside from unauthorized use; WARD separates recognizing clinical evidence from updating state and acting; LeanReviewBench separates a checked formal proof from correspondence with the intended informal statement. A model checking its own picture is not an independent verifier. Editorial synthesis. Siren · WARD · LeanReviewBench · SenseNova

KAT-2 — Agents / frameworks / CORE-AI

#
  • E04 — HyperHarness amortizes personalization. A shared generator writes user-specific retrieval and answer-guidance code from permitted histories, with one-pass construction for unseen users rather than deployment-time task-scored search. Across three benchmarks and three answer models it leads seven of nine settings, with gains up to 5.95 points over the strongest baselines. 21 November preprint; HIGH abstract/history. Limits: the reported 90.32% reduction is matched answer-time tokens versus long-context prompting, not all training/setup cost, latency or generated-code security. Paper
  • E05 — ContextFact preserves the context that retrieval alone cannot recover. Context-preserving facts link to source-grounded entity representations reconstructed across sessions. With Qwen3.5-27B, authors report 86.49% LoCoMo and 91.00% LongMemEval-S judge accuracy while consuming 21% of the strongest evaluated baseline's evidence tokens. 22 November preprint. Limits: benchmark/judge accuracy is not perfect personal recall, privacy assurance or a 79% reduction in every cost. Paper

#

Limit: this is a benchmark and reference-label construction process, not a new theorem or verification of a particular current proof claim. Paper

Explicit boundary descriptions reduce reaching by a relative 68–96%. 24 November; HIGH methodology/results. Limits: safeguards-off models and deliberately tempting scenarios, not production prevalence. Why it matters: screenshot instruction filtering alone can miss destination risk. Limit: a benchmark/configuration result, not 92% of all internet agents compromised or a live incident. Paper

AI-SCIENCE

  • E09 — RumiFold links protein structure and natural-language function. A chat model predicts structures from hidden states and uses repeated folding passes to answer function questions. Authors report folding comparable to single-representation models such as SimpleFold and strong function prediction. 22 November preprint; HIGH abstract/history. Sections 15

INFRA

#
  • E08 — KernelShift requires testing hardware evolution. Ninety-nine real-repository kernels span Neon/SVE/SVE2/SME2. Agents beat auto-vectorized compilers by 2.4–19.8× on established extensions but reach only 0.70× hand-tuned KleidiAI. Two LLMs show separate 1.3–1.6× prefill gains. 22 November preprint. Limits: unreliable adaptation to unfamiliar SME2 models; kernel/prefill speed is not whole-generation speed or total billing savings. Paper
  • E14 — DECAL evaluates diffusion-LLM quantization with its deployed decoder. W4A4 errors concentrate in forced commitments. Compressed logit gaps can preserve predictions while adding forward passes: a parallelism tax. Limits: LLaDA/Dream and compared W4A4 methods; no numerical speedup in the abstract, no universal free acceleration. Paper
