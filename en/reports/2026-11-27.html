Sections 03

# 1. GitHub will change the invoice default on Copilot code review accounts whose selection is not saved.

#

Why it matters: According to GitHub's November 25 announcement, reviews of licensed members under Copilot Business and Enterprise will be written to the organization that owns the repository, starting December 30, 2026, if the billing preference is not saved. This is an admin setting that affects the team budget. Who it touches: Developer / Administrator How sure: Certain What you can do: If you want to protect the member from being deducted, as an authorized administrator, explicitly record the Member selection in the Copilot policy. Caution: Previously saved preferences are preserved; There are exceptions for accounts with paid AI Credits disabled or billing blocked. Source: GitHub announcement

Sections 04

# 2. Rackspace Spot's closing date was announced as December 8th.

#

Why it matters: Rackspace's November 24 notice says the Spot service will shut down at 5 PM US Central Time on December 8, 2026, and running workloads will be terminated. The Register's Nov. 26 story cites the need for customers to update their migration and access information following the security incident. Who it touches: Developer / Administrator How sure: Certain What you can do: If you're using Spot, start the migration plan based on the data retrieval deadline in the customer notification. Attention: The service closing time does not replace the reported data retrieval deadline for your account. Source: The Register, official status statement

Sections 05

# 3. 1Password announced that company systems were infiltrated and some customer information was copied.

#

Why it matters: According to the company's Nov. 25 statement, systems were accessed through an employee's GitHub account; Some customers' names, emails and account information were copied. The company says the contents and keys of password vaults are not affected. Who it touches: Public / Administrator How sure: Company claim What you can do: Verify unexpected invitation or account recovery messages through your own account without going to the link in the message. Attention: Evaluation regarding safe security is the company's statement; It is not the result of a completed independent technical review. Source: The Register, 1Password description

Sections 06

# 4. Microsoft reported that the artificial intelligence tool called ARTEX was used to automate attack steps.

#

Why it matters: The November 26 news report, citing Microsoft's November 25 analysis, says that state-sponsored and financially motivated groups are automating reconnaissance and system infiltration steps. In defense, it becomes important to notice when an account moves into different attack phases in a short time. Who it touches: Developer / Administrator How sure: Company claim What you can do: Check with your security team to see if a variety of reconnaissance and access attempts are detected in the logs. Attention: The analysis does not describe a new vulnerability technique, but rather the automation of existing attack techniques. Source: The Decoder, Microsoft analysis

Sections 07

# 5. Released a panel comparing metrics on AMD hardware for llama.cpp.

#

Why it matters: The November 26 announcement provides a first comparison of model, software version, and numerical sensitivity options on the Radeon 8060S and Radeon AI PRO R9700S. The workflow is open to everyone, with metrics showing separate input processing and response generation speeds. Who it touches: Developer / Student How sure: Certain What you can do: Compare two speed measurements separately for the same model and sensitivity on the panel before selecting a local model. Caution: The results of these two devices are not the result of a comparison with all AMD hardware or other brands. Source: llama.cpp–AMD panel announcement

Sections 08

# 6. NVIDIA has released additional training data and acceleration recipe for Nemotron 3.5 Parse.

#

Why it matters: The November 26 package includes synthetic data, an additional training recipe for the model, and the DFlash2 accelerator for processing graphics, tables, and multilingual text in documents. NVIDIA reported 7.62x speedup under a single request condition in testing processing the same 2,078 pages on a single H200. Who it touches: Developer / Administrator How sure: Company claim What you can do: If you have a document processing project, compare the published training recipe to your own document types. Attention: The reported earnings decrease to 1.52 times on 512 concurrent requests and do not indicate Turkish accuracy. Source: NVIDIA&#x27;s additional package article

Sections 09

Only try of the day

#

Open the free llama.cpp–AMD panel guide and compare two speed measurements of the model every 15 minutes. Distinguish which work the numbers measure by noting in the guide that the PP512 is a test of processing 512 inputs, while the TG128 is a test of producing 128 outputs without previous context.
