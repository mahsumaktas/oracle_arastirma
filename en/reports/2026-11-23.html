Summary of the Day

#

The most concrete security finding of the night is that the model name and ZDR label alone do not prove the actual bus: Dragos' canary research and statements from OpenRouter, Together and Novita reveal different leakage/access mechanisms. OpenAI, on the other hand, proposes to track frontier development with a common perplexity metric and set boundaries tied to security milestones; The text also explains its own problems of syllogism. The highlights of the research selection are the agent's improvement of the physical system by measuring it, testing the human-LLM referee exchange, and evaluating reasoning and robot execution together. Editorial synthesis: The intermediate indicator should not be confused with the final result: the ZDR label is not data-path control, annotator agreement is not accuracy, and individual ability scores are not composite task success. #
  • E07 — How much of the Diffusion RL gains are fixed activation shift? The same steering vectors are added to the block outputs of the Frozen base model. 0.29M vector parameters / 18.8M LoRA parameters in SD3.5-M, i.e. approximately 65 times difference.   • E08 — “A CPU Inside an LLM”: analytical circuit graft instead of learned general reasoning. Compiling specific arithmetic/Boolean circuits to weights with zero gradient and training example. 99.17% / 100% ALU-prefix accuracy for Granite 4.2 3B and Qwen 3.5 4B in the six-digit, N=1200 BF16 experiment; The FP32 sidecar result is different. Limit: This is not 100% accurate on all the math or a general purpose OS CPU representation. Zero training instances does not mean zero additional parameters/memory/compilation work. Strong technical claims are not reproduced in this report. 19 November v1. Source
  • E09 — AltRA-Test: test that does not require the same annotators to see every sample. Comparing Bootstrap with an all-human panel and a panel that replaces a human with LLM; The target is inter-annotator agreement to be non-inferior. 18 LLM × 5 prompting × 20 datasets; Text, image, audio and video coverage. It is reported that approximately 100 items are sufficient for most experiments, and passage is reported in approximately half of the settings. Limit: Maintaining an agreement does not prove ground truth or substitution of every human role; Evaluation is required on a task/model basis. 19 November v1. Source
  • E10 — ITPD: local-to-global parent discovery in nonstationary time series. Sound/complete procedure under Exact oracle; F1–time Pareto advantage in simulation benchmarks. Reported speed of 2.3× / 9.1× / 40.9× according to iterativeIAMB, DF-PC and PCMCI at 2000 node setting. Limit: Hypothetical causal discovery and specific simulation; observational is not unconditional causality from real data or 40.9× gain on each graph. November 19 v1; preprint. Source
