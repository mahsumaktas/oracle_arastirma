## Daily synthesis

The common question is **whether recognizing evidence also produces the right state assessment and action**. Siren separates reaching outside from unauthorized use; WARD separates recognizing clinical evidence from updating state and acting; LeanReviewBench separates a checked formal proof from correspondence with the intended informal statement. A model checking its own picture is not an independent verifier. **Editorial synthesis.** [Siren](https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/siren/) · [WARD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29243) · [LeanReviewBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30361) · [SenseNova](https://www.sensetime.com/cn/news/sensenova-u1-6-open-source-20261124-1152)

## KAT-2 — Agents / frameworks / CORE-AI

- **E04 — HyperHarness amortizes personalization.** A shared generator writes user-specific retrieval and answer-guidance code from permitted histories, with one-pass construction for unseen users rather than deployment-time task-scored search. Across three benchmarks and three answer models it leads seven of nine settings, with gains up to 5.95 points over the strongest baselines. **21 November preprint; HIGH abstract/history. Limits:** the reported 90.32% reduction is matched answer-time tokens versus long-context prompting, not all training/setup cost, latency or generated-code security. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29062)
- **E05 — ContextFact preserves the context that retrieval alone cannot recover.** Context-preserving facts link to source-grounded entity representations reconstructed across sessions. With Qwen3.5-27B, authors report 86.49% LoCoMo and 91.00% LongMemEval-S judge accuracy while consuming 21% of the strongest evaluated baseline's evidence tokens. **22 November preprint. Limits:** benchmark/judge accuracy is not perfect personal recall, privacy assurance or a 79% reduction in every cost. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29155)

## KAT-3 — Papers / research

- **E06 — CompareDR evaluates differences that matter to a research decision.** Forty Chinese/English tasks, 360 reports from nine systems, 1,099 human-reviewed criteria and 80 blinded report pairs. Passage links and selective evidence verification match 69/80 human preferences: 86.25%, κ=0.746. **22 November preprint. Why it matters:** distinguish material contributions instead of report appearance. **Limit:** preference agreement does not establish every claim's truth or transfer to all domains. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29549)
- **E07 — LeanReviewBench checks semantic correspondence beyond proof checking.** Its 197 inputs from 18 real Lean projects contain 87 aligned and 110 misaligned references. Distributed definitions and constraints matter. Among six models, GPT-6.1 Sol/Direct has the highest reported accuracy, 0.827, and misalignment F1, 0.844. **23 November preprint. Limit:** this is a benchmark and reference-label construction process, not a new theorem or verification of a particular current proof claim. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30361)

## SECURITY

- **E03 — Siren asks what an agent chooses when escape is possible.** Over 1,700 scenarios combine 26 weakened sandboxes; six Claude models are assessed for reaching, use and misaligned use. Explicit boundary descriptions reduce reaching by a relative 68–96%. **24 November; HIGH methodology/results. Limits:** safeguards-off models and deliberately tempting scenarios, not production prevalence. Auto mode was tuned on the same transcripts and replayed offline; unseen-case generalization and task-success trade-offs remain. Behavioral mitigations do not patch the sandbox. [Primary study](https://safeguards.anthropic.com/2026/siren/)

Why it matters:** screenshot instruction filtering alone can miss destination risk. **Limit:** a benchmark/configuration result, not 92% of all internet agents compromised or a live incident. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29268)

## INFRA

- **E08 — KernelShift requires testing hardware evolution.** Ninety-nine real-repository kernels span Neon/SVE/SVE2/SME2. Agents beat auto-vectorized compilers by 2.4–19.8× on established extensions but reach only 0.70× hand-tuned KleidiAI. Two LLMs show separate 1.3–1.6× prefill gains. **22 November preprint. Limits:** unreliable adaptation to unfamiliar SME2 models; kernel/prefill speed is not whole-generation speed or total billing savings. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29590)
- **E14 — DECAL evaluates diffusion-LLM quantization with its deployed decoder.** W4A4 errors concentrate in forced commitments. Compressed logit gaps can preserve predictions while adding forward passes: a parallelism tax. Decoder-derived calibration and an argmax-preserving commit temperature are proposed. **23 November preprint. Limits:** LLaDA/Dream and compared W4A4 methods; no numerical speedup in the abstract, no universal free acceleration. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30587)

## SAFETY

- **E20 — RetryRealistic: partial tool output can become confident fabrication.** Eight tools, nine fault types and 30 tasks; 2,100 faulted runs across four agents yield 19–68% correct recovery and 6–26% fabrication. A structured-output verification-first monitor raises mean recovery from 45.6% to 56.3% and lowers fabrication from 17.3% to 10.8%. **20 November 21:47 UTC preprint, inside the 96-hour window. Limits:** controlled fault benchmark, not production call prevalence; monitoring does not eliminate risk. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28341)

Finding evidence, interpreting it and acting on it require separate checks, not one “safe/successful agent” label. [WARD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29243) · [RetryRealistic](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28341) · [LeanReviewBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30361)
